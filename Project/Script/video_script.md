# السكريبت: علم نفس الناس الذين يحلمون كثيراً ولا يفعلون شيئاً

**المدة الإجمالية:** 75-85 ثانية
**الجمهور المستهدف:** 18-35 سنة / محبو علم النفس والتطوير الذاتي
**الأسلوب:** محادثاتي، عاطفي، مقنع، سريع الإيقاع

---

## السكريبت الكامل (بالإنجليزية للفيديو)

---

**[HOOK - 0:00 to 0:05]**
You know that person who always talks about their big dreams... but never actually does anything?

**[0:05 to 0:12]**
Yeah. That might be you. And here's the uncomfortable truth...

**[0:12 to 0:20]**
Your brain is literally working against you. When you imagine success, your brain releases dopamine — the same chemical released when you actually achieve something.

**[0:20 to 0:27]**
So you get the reward without doing the work. And your brain? It's perfectly happy with that.

**[0:27 to 0:35]**
Then comes the fear. Not just fear of failure. But fear of actually succeeding. Because success means change. And change is terrifying.

**[0:35 to 0:42]**
So instead, you plan. You research. You wait for the perfect moment. That moment never comes.

**[0:42 to 0:50]**
This is called analysis paralysis. You're so busy thinking about doing the thing that you never actually do the thing.

**[0:50 to 0:58]**
And here's the deepest part: You've built your entire identity around your dreams. "I'm going to be an entrepreneur." "I'm going to write that book." But identity without action is just a story.

**[0:58 to 1:05]**
The fix isn't motivation. It's not another YouTube video. It's one tiny action. Right now. Today.

**[1:05 to 1:15]**
Because the people who succeed don't wait to feel ready. They act first. And then they feel ready.

**[1:15 to 1:20]**
Stop dreaming about your life. Start building it.

- الإيقاع: سريع في المقدمة، يتباطأ عند المفاهيم العاطفية، يتسارع عند النهاية
- نبرة الصوت: مباشرة، جدية لكن غير متعجرفة، متعاطفة
- توقفات درامية: بعد "that might be you" وبعد "your brain is perfectly happy with that"
