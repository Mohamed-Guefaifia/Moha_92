## الوصف (Description)

```
Most people will work 40 years and retire with almost nothing. Not because they weren't smart. But because of how their brain thinks about money. 🧠💸

In this video, we break down the psychology behind why most people stay poor:

• Scarcity mindset — how growing up without money programs your brain (Mullainathan & Shafir)
• Lifestyle inflation — why earning more doesn't make you richer
• The lottery mentality trap — waiting for overnight success that never comes
• Delayed gratification — the #1 habit that separates those who escape poverty
• Financial identity — why "I'm not a money person" keeps you broke
• Loss aversion — why fear of losing stops you from investing (Kahneman)

💡 Awareness is the first step. You can't change a pattern you can't see.
```
