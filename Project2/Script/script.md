# Script: Why Most People Stay Poor (Psychology)

**Duration:** ~62 seconds
**Voice Style:** Deep, direct, slightly uncomfortable, empathetic
**Hook:** First 5 seconds — shocking statistic

---

## FULL SCRIPT

**[HOOK — 0:00–0:05]**
Most people will work 40 years... and retire with almost nothing.

**[0:05–0:10]**
And it's not because they weren't smart. Or didn't work hard enough.

**[0:10–0:17]**
It's because of how their brain is wired to think about money.

**[0:17–0:26]**
When you grow up without money, your brain develops a scarcity mindset. You think small. You focus on surviving today — not building tomorrow.

**[0:26–0:33]**
Then comes lifestyle inflation. Every time you earn more... you spend more. The gap between income and wealth never closes.

**[0:33–0:40]**
And here's the trap: most people wait for a big break. A lucky moment. Something that will change everything overnight.

**[0:40–0:46]**
But wealth isn't built in moments. It's built in boring, invisible daily decisions.

**[0:46–0:54]**
The people who escape poverty don't just work harder. They delay gratification. They invest instead of spend. They treat money like a skill to learn.

**[0:54–1:00]**
Because here's the uncomfortable truth: staying poor isn't just bad luck. It's a pattern your brain keeps repeating... without telling you.

**[1:00–1:05]**
Awareness is the first exit. You can't change what you can't see.

---

## Director's Notes
- Pause after "almost nothing" for 0.5 seconds
- Speed up slightly during "lifestyle inflation" section
- Slow down and lower tone on "staying poor isn't just bad luck"
- End with firm, direct tone
